# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of The Gold Standard — everyday people (not athletes) who bought a 12-week structured fitness program from Hannah Goldy. They arrive motivated but time-poor, often training alone at a commercial gym with a phone in hand. Mid-workout they need form checks in seconds; at home they watch lessons on a laptop. Many are beginners intimidated by gyms; the interface must never add to that intimidation. Secondary user: Hannah/admin uploading content via Drive file IDs.

## Product Purpose

The member area delivers the purchased course: 3 tracks × 20 video lessons plus a 72-movement exercise demo library. Success = members find today's lesson in under 5 seconds, complete the 12 weeks (retention → testimonials → upsells), and check exercise form mid-workout without friction. It also carries the coaching upsell path (1-on-1 and group coaching) at natural moments.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, athletic, authentic. Speaks like a coach who's done it: direct, motivating without toxic positivity, no fluff. The interface should feel like premium equipment — solid, dark, gold-accented — while staying invisible during the task. Champion energy, zero clutter.

## Anti-references

- Generic LMS clutter (Teachable/Thinkific default themes): busy sidebars, badge spam, progress gamification overload.
- Aggressive clashing colors and shouting typography of discount fitness sites (competitor anti-pattern from brand research).
- Cinematic overproduction — Hannah explicitly chose "clarity over cinematic" for the video content; the UI must match that restraint.
- Anything that exposes the plumbing (Google Drive links/branding) — content must read as proprietary.

## Design Principles

1. **The workout is the hero** — every screen exists to get the member to press play; nothing competes with the video.
2. **Five-second wayfinding** — a sweaty member with 60 seconds of rest finds any exercise demo in five seconds.
3. **Earned familiarity** — Kajabi-class patterns members already know; novelty is spent on brand, never on navigation.
4. **Coach's voice, everywhere** — UI copy sounds like Hannah: direct, warm, no corporate filler.
5. **Progress you can feel** — completion state is always visible but never nags.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Members use it mid-workout on phones: large touch targets (≥44px), high contrast on dark surfaces (≥4.5:1 body text), visible keyboard focus, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support, captions on all videos (.srt via Drive player). No information conveyed by color alone.
