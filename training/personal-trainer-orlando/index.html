One-On-One, In Person

A Female Personal Trainer In Orlando.

When we train together, it's just the two of us. I build your plan around your goals and your schedule, and I'm right there through every set to coach your form and adjust things as you get stronger.

Your plan belongs to you, so it can change when your life does. A rough week or a sore shoulder just means we adjust and keep going.

If training with another woman helps you feel more at ease, I completely get that. Men are welcome to train with me too.

I keep my client list small on purpose. It means I know how your week went and what your body is telling you, so the coaching stays personal.

My Story

Why I Coach This Way.

I've had hypothyroidism since I was ten, so I know how discouraging it feels to work hard while your body seems to drag its feet. After I had my son, Odin, I was 180 pounds. I got down to 115 and fought six months later.

Getting there took patience and a plan I could actually stick with. That's what I want to build with you, whether this is your first time with a trainer or your fifth fresh start.
