Who It's For

Women's Jiu-Jitsu In Orlando.

Walking into a packed class for the first time can feel like a lot. If you're a woman who wants to try jiu-jitsu in Orlando but would rather learn one-on-one first, a private lesson is a comfortable way to begin. Guys are welcome to book privates too.

Privates also make sense if you already train and want extra time on a position that keeps giving you trouble, or if you have a competition coming up.

Maybe you simply want to feel more confident knowing you can handle yourself. That's a great reason to start, and you don't need any experience. We'll begin where you are and build from there at your pace.

Learn From A UFC Veteran

Private MMA Training.

I've been in MMA for 12 years and fought professionally for 10 of them, including in the UFC. In a private MMA session, we spend our time on the parts of the game you want to work on, at a pace that makes sense for you.

Your lessons are built around your goals, whether it's your first time putting on gloves or you're sharpening skills you already have. Because it's just us, we can slow down and drill a move as many times as you need. Questions are always welcome.

I'm also a NASM Certified Personal Trainer, and I keep my client list small, so you'll always be working with me directly.
