Built For Your Group

A Self-Defense Seminar That Fits.

Before we set a date, we talk. I'll want to know who's coming and what you'd like everyone to walk away with, and then I plan a hands-on session around that.

A room full of first-timers needs something very different from a team that trains every week, so no two seminars look exactly alike. If some people in your group feel nervous about the physical side, tell me ahead of time and I'll plan for it.

For Women

Self-Defense Classes For Women In Orlando.

If you're organizing a self-defense class for women in Orlando, maybe for a moms' group or a group of coworkers, I'd love to teach it. I'm a mom myself, and my goal is for every woman in the room to leave feeling more sure of herself.

Mixed groups are welcome as well, and I bring the same approach to groups in Dallas.

For Gyms

Jiu-Jitsu & MMA Seminars.

Gym owners can bring me in for a jiu-jitsu seminar or an MMA session with their members. I'm a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt and spent 10 years as a pro fighter, including time in the UFC, so we can get as technical as your team wants.

Newer members won't get lost either. I pitch the material to the experience level in the room, which we'll talk through ahead of time.

Travel Available

Outside Orlando Or Dallas?

I'm happy to travel to your gym or event. Tell me where you are and how big your group is, and we'll work out the plan together. Whether it's a big event or a small team, the first step is the same: a free call.

Looking for one-on-one time instead? I also teach private jiu-jitsu and MMA lessons.
