One-On-One In Dallas

Training With A Female Personal Trainer In Dallas.

I know fitness has to fit into real life. We'll look at your goals and what your week really looks like, then build a plan you can keep up with.

In our sessions I'll push you when it's time to push, and I'll listen when something doesn't feel right. Your plan changes as you get stronger.

You don't need to get in shape before you start working with me. Our first sessions are where I learn how you move, and we build from there.

If you'd feel more comfortable working out with a woman, I understand, and men are just as welcome to book with me.

My Background

Where I'm Coming From.

I've spent 12 years in MMA, 10 of them as a pro, including fighting in the UFC. I'm a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt and a NASM Certified Personal Trainer, and I keep my client list small so each person gets my real attention.

My own fitness hasn't always been simple. I've lived with hypothyroidism since age ten, and after my son Odin was born I weighed 180 pounds. I got down to 115 and fought six months later. Whether you're starting over or starting for the very first time, you'll get zero judgment from me.

Service Area
