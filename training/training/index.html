About Me

A Little About Me.

I've been training MMA for over twelve years, ten of them professionally, including competing in the UFC. I'm a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt and NASM Certified Personal Trainer, but my relationship with health and fitness hasn't always been simple.

I was diagnosed with hypothyroidism at ten, so I understand how frustrating it can feel when you're putting in the work and your body doesn't seem to cooperate. After having my son, I was 180 pounds. Six months later, I stepped back into the cage at 115. Getting there wasn't about a quick fix. It took patience, consistency, and a plan that worked for me.

That's a big part of why I love coaching. Everyone's starting point is different, and the right plan should meet you there. If you've tried before and it didn't stick, you're exactly the kind of person I'd love to work with.
